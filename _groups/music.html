I compose and produce my own music as a hobby. I've collected collected many of my creations here, as well as on my Youtube Channel.

My musical endeavors began with piano lessons from 3rd grade through high school. One of the two recitals we had each year were our own compositions, so I've been pushed to write music since I was young. However, it wasn't until the summer before college that I actually found the intrinsic motivation to continue making music. This is when I started exploring digital music using FL Studio. I've also used MuseScore to create sheet music for my piano solo pieces.
